Black evening.
White snow.
Wind, wind!
Man can't stand on his feet.
Wind, wind —
In all of God's creation!
The wind is blowing
White snowflakes.
There's ice under the snow.
It's slippery, it's hard,
Every walker
Slides — oh, poor thing!
 From building to building,
A rope is stretched.
There's a poster on the rope:
"All power to the Constituent Assembly!"
The old woman is crying,
She can't understand what it means,
What's the point of such a poster,
Such a huge piece of cloth?
How many foot wrappings it would take for the kids,
But everyone is naked and shoeless.
